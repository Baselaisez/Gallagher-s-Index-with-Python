Ziyade bablardan Muhtelife cekimi (model: AKRAMA, Form IV)
==========================================================
Source: user upload 2026-07-29 (.xls). The full muhtelife recitation for an augmented bab,
including the extension rows (masdar mimi/zaman/mekan, binai merre & nev', ismi mensup,
tafdil paraphrase, taaccub) and the rule that ismi alet/tasgir/mubalaga do not come
from ziyade babs. Cell order is right-to-left per row.
