# last, the rest ḍamma; muḍāriʿ majhūl in four steps; amr: drop the muḍāraʿa letter, waṣl hamza by the ʿayn's vowel, Form IV keeps its
# fatḥa hamza), the two tāʾs of V/VI, the iftiʿāl tāʾ rules (ṭ after ṣ ḍ ṭ ẓ; d after z dh d; w/y/th → t with idghām), nūn al-tawkīd

# ism fāʿil/mafʿūl tables, the three idghām rulings (wājib/jāʾiz/mumtaniʿ), the weak classes (mithāl, ajwaf, nāqiṣ, lafīf maqrūn/mafrūq)

lakabu olan İzzddin

nazma dökme

yakın çalışmalar
mevcuttur.
