Avamil-i Curcani — slide deck (Ozkan Yaman), 80 pages
============================================================
Source: Avamili_C_rc_n_.pdf (user upload, 2026-07-31). A Turkish
teaching deck on the Awamil of Abd al-Qahir al-Jurjani (d. 1078):
Arabic matn slides with Turkish framing, part 1 of a series.

The classification carried by the deck (read from pages 1-10):
- 100 amil in all (الْعَوَامِلُ فِي النَّحْوِ ... مِئَةُ عَامِلٍ).
- They divide into LAFZI and MANEVI; the lafzi into SEMA'I (91) and
  KIYASI (7); the manevi are 2.
- The sema'i come in 13 kinds (تَتَنَوَّعُ عَلَى ثَلَاثَةَ عَشَرَ نَوْعًا).
- Kind 1: the letters that put a SINGLE noun in jarr — SEVENTEEN
  (حُرُوفٌ تَجُرُّ الِاسْمَ الْوَاحِدَ فَقَطْ، وَهِيَ سَبْعَةَ عَشَرَ حَرْفًا).
- The deck then walks the 17 one by one with the meanings each adds
  (بِ: ilsak — مَرَرْتُ بِزَيْدٍ "Zeyd'e ugradim"; etc.).

The full particle-by-particle walk (pages 10-80) remains in the PDF;
transcribe further sections as features need them. This deck is the
Jurjani counterpart to the Birgivi Awamil already modeled in the
