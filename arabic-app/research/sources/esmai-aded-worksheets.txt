Esma-i aded — the number-name worksheets (أسماء عدد)
============================================================
Source: esmai_aded___1.doc and esmai_aded__2.doc (user upload,
2026-07-31). Two scanned handwritten madrasah worksheets titled
أسماء عدد: a figure in one column, its composed Arabic number-name in
the other. Sheet 1 runs 125–1133, sheet 2 runs 1100–3000. The scans are
JPEG pages embedded in the Word files; the pairs below are transcribed
from the scans (unvocalized, as handwritten). They are the specification
and the TEST TABLE for the app's Aded engine (adadName in
prototype/reader.html): composition order is thousands، hundreds، units،
tens — units BEFORE tens — joined by و; hundreds multiples written
joined (ثلاثمائة); 11–19 as one block.
