## partPlace — harf (6)

A line that opens «حرف …» carries «في محل …». In every case found the place belongs to the OTHER word of the token (a jarr letter and its pronoun / «مَا» written as one token, the two parsed in one clause without a comma) — the reader's limit, not an author's slip; the chain yields. A comma between the two clauses (as the other lines have) would let the chain explain both.

What the rule would say: Iẓhār pp. 115–116 «القسم الأول ما لا يكون معمولا أصلا … الأول الحرف مطلقا» — a particle itself has no place
