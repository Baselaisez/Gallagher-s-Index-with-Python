## Read but not kept, and what the books do not say

- From Ibn Mālik's sections of the fāʿil, the nāʾib, the pronouns, the demonstratives and the five verbs the candidates were read; what was kept is about order, deletion and the follower's case — NEITHER Ibn Mālik's bāb al-fāʿil NOR Shaykhzāde states why the fāʿil itself is rafʿ, so the chain's «family → state» step for the fāʿil has no book reason (the reason the owner's own example asks for lives in books like Ibn al-Anbārī's Asrār al-ʿArabiyya — not received).
- How much of the cut was READ: Shaykhzāde — the 193 candidates (of 379) that name an iʿrāb term; Ibn Mālik — per WAVE topic the shortest 14–25 (about 260 of 350); al-Raḍī — the 26 under 33 words, 23 of 33–70 words in the core iʿlāl / idghām / hamza sections and a search for the classic formulae (about 60 of 255). The rest of the cut is the next round's reading list (research/ilal/candidates.json).
- Ibn Mālik's signs chapter (1:174–221) is terse: 25 candidates (most about the jussive jawāb and the asmāʾ al-afʿāl), 6 kept.
